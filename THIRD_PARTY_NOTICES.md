## Codebox Homoglyph

The file `adversarial/data/codebox_ascii_homoglyphs.txt` is an adapted,
ASCII-anchored subset of character groups from
[Codebox Homoglyph](https://github.com/codebox/homoglyph). The subset keeps
practical Basic Multilingual Plane characters and omits supplementary-plane
mathematical alphabets.

Source data: https://github.com/codebox/homoglyph/blob/master/raw_data/chars.txt
